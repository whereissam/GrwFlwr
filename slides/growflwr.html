Every figure is computed before the model is called. The agent explains numbers; it does not invent them.

What we are not claiming07 / 09

Three things a judge
should hear from us first.

The data is generated

Synthetic, from a written spec. Our numbers say how well the model recovers a generator's rules — not how it would advise a real farmer. Real error would be larger.

One farm pays

farm_4 holds the most data and loses 0.020 macro-F1 by joining. Three gain, one pays. That is why the authority owns the model, not the largest farm.

77% is 10 of 13

High-need recall rests on thirteen held-out rows. It is a direction, not a precise figure. More High events is our first data ask.
