Why this exists

Drought is
a survival
problem.

Europe's farmers face rising water costs and hard pumping limits. Efficient irrigation stopped being an optimisation and became a condition of staying in business.

AND YET
Nobody will
upload the data.

Pumping logs, soil quality and yield are commercial secrets. Worse, raw extraction records expose a farm to fines and water-rights disputes.

ZERO

farmers join a centralised platform. That is the deadlock every smart-agriculture AI has hit.

Not over-engineering
Federation is the
only way in.

If privacy were not the barrier, a central database would be simpler and we would have built one. Privacy is the barrier. Flower unlocks data that otherwise does not exist.

Measured: pooling everything centrally scores macro-F1 0.722; federated scores 0.736. Keeping data home costs nothing.

GrowFlwr04 — The product
Where this goes05 / 05

Water is allocated
regionally.
So should the model be.

The data is generated

Synthetic, from a written spec. Our numbers show a model recovering a generator's rules — real-world error would be larger.

Two farms is very few

FedAvg over two clients is a noisy average. An earlier four-farm split had one farm losing by joining.

68% is not enough

We catch 30 of 44 urgent days. Central pooling catches slightly more. We are not ahead on the class that matters most.
