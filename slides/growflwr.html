Also true
Federated ≈
centralized.

Pooling every row into one model scores macro-F1 0.722; federated scores 0.736. On High-need recall centralized is ahead, 70% to 68%.

Neither is clearly better. That is the point: keeping the data home costs essentially nothing.

Every figure is computed before the model is called. The agent explains numbers; it does not invent them.

What we are not claiming07 / 09

Three things a judge
should hear from us first.

The data is generated

Synthetic, from a written spec. Our numbers say how well the model recovers a generator's rules — not how it would advise a real farmer. Real error would be larger.

Two farms is very few

FedAvg over two clients is a noisy average. An earlier four-farm version had one farm losing by joining — whether everyone gains depends on how specialised the farms are.

High recall is 68%

Of 44 High days in the 2026 season, the model catches 30. Pooling the data centrally catches more — 77%. We are not ahead on the class that matters most.
